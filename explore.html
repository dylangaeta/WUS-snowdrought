Compare variables

Overlay standardized anomalies (σ) for up to 3 variables on one chart to see how they move together over time — e.g. precipitation, soil moisture, and vegetation response. All series share one axis because σ removes each variable's own physical units, leaving only how far each one departs from its own baseline.
