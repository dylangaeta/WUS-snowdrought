Snow drought classification by winter type

Dierauer, Allen & Whitfield (2019, Water Resources Research) classification: dry / warm-and-dry / warm snow drought, by precipitation and temperature departure. One point per DJFM winter (1991–2026). Color = classification (temperature × precipitation × snowpack), independent of axes shown.

Descriptive only -- no product is combined with another. Source: code/11_context_SnowDroughtQuadrants_analyze.py's own classification table.
