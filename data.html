Regional summary vs. fixed calendar-month climatology

Computed on demand from the same monthly grid-cell-detrended values as the product explorer — pick any month or season window, any year, and which value to display. Color = each variable's own physical direction of stress (not raw arithmetic sign) — stress (drier/hotter/less productive) vs. relief. Native standardized indices (SPI/SPEI/EDDI/PDSI/ForDRI/ESI) already are their own fixed-timescale statistic (e.g. SPI-03 is inherently a trailing 3-month index), so for a season window they show their own reading as of that window's last month rather than being re-aggregated across it — labeled "as of <month>" when that applies. Percent-of-normal is not shown for them, and is omitted wherever a baseline mean near zero (e.g. some temperature/VPD anomaly fields) would make it unstable.

Anomaly heatmaps by product and time

Every product's standardized anomaly (σ) from its own baseline, one row per product/response, one column per month or year depending on the period selected below -- computed live the same way as every other chart on this dashboard, not a static image. Colored by stress direction, not raw sign: red always means more stress and blue always means less, so a row is sign-flipped when its own physical variable's stress direction is negative (e.g. low snowpack, not high).
